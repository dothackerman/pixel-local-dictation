# ADR-011 — Separate quality, resources and completeness

2026-10-14 · Implemented diagnostic contract; acceptance unchanged.

Match platform/build/input/feed/process/timing controls and actual runtime/native hashes.
Dictionary acceptance needs genuine twins; allocation diagnostics grant no mechanism acceptance.
Missing evidence is incomplete; resource failure still reports quality.
Separate fresh unpaced, fresh paced, persistent one-buffer and historical-full modes.
Report RSS/PSS, high-water and available Java/native phases; new counters cannot replace old gates.
Checksum-warmed fresh load is not cold filesystem cache; node durations are not wall time.
Freeze varied Stop prefixes; report all waits, median/max, missing/duplicate tails and audible references.
Small suites establish no population p95; feeds are not independent speech samples.
Freeze repeats/order, thermal conditions and the measured-run procedure.
Validate every run/barrier before downstream inference; preserve failed evidence and immutable identities.
Stop affected work on invalid provenance/coverage, truncation, missing output, denied access or decoder failure.
Preserve failed and partial attempts; reconcile unknown execution before retrying.
Handback: identities, attempts/deviations, per-language quality, guards/resources, backend/fallback and verified restoration.
Completion, evidence quality, candidate acceptance and deployment are distinct.
Revisit metrics/thresholds through explicit versioned protocol; historical verdicts stand.
