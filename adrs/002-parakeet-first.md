# ADR-002 — Parakeet-first prototype

2026-10-09 · Proposal; ADR-003 later approved the working engine.

Prototype one multilingual Parakeet CPU recognizer behind FUTO’s voice IME.
Prove launch/insertion, then recognition on the actual phone; tags m1/m2 demonstrate operation.
One model avoids a router; short-utterance detection, load, latency and heat remain risks.
Approval does not extend to every original proposal or permanent deployment.
Revisit on R1/R4 failure or a better complete local path.
