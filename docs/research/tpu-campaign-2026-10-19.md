## Official route and concrete access blocker

- Google supports Tensor G5 through LiteRT **AOT CompiledModel**; Google Tensor JIT compilation is not supported. Android NPU runtime package from 2.3.0: `com.google.ai.edge.litert:litert-npu-runtime-google-tensor:2.3.0`, alongside `com.google.ai.edge.litert:litert:2.3.0`. Fallback and partial delegation are supported; invocation success proves neither full delegation nor TPU execution. [Official NPU guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/next/npu).
- Official compatibility: Tensor SDK 2.1, LiteRT 2.3.0 or nightly 2.3.0.dev20261001, Tensor G5 Android 26Q3. Check actual device compatibility before use; no device inspection was performed here. [Release matrix](https://developers.google.com/edge/tensor-sdk/release-notes).
- Official compiler requires static shapes and can introduce numerical issues. Stable compiler flow requires `GOOGLE_TENSOR_BACKEND_ENABLED=1`. [Compiler FAQ](https://developers.google.com/edge/tensor-sdk/tensor-faqs).
- **INT8 is not established as necessary.** Compiler supports `google_tensor_truncation_type` values `auto`, `bfloat16`, `half`, `no_truncation`; default `auto` cannot substantiate FP32 preservation. Explicit `no_truncation` is the first precision contract to investigate, followed by numerical and recognition checks. Do not equate an FP32 input/output interface with internal FP32 execution. [Official flags](https://developers.google.com/edge/tensor-sdk/compilation-flags).
