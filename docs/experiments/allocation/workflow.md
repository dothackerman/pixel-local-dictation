## Frozen design

Stage one preserves the [legacy runbook](stage-1-runbook.md), all eighteen planned processes and eleven comparison barriers, inputs, timing, repeats and thresholds. The aggregate namespace gives those runs `s1-` identities and unique output files. The pre-layout contracts and original runbook are retained in `corpus/lab/release-1a/pre-doc-layout-20261014/`; raw setup-smoke reports retain their measured identities. Legacy execution still uses its original wall-clock accounting. The combined helper implements explicit active-work intervals under a distinct ledger schema; it does not relabel prior execution.

Stages two and three each have four variants: the app baseline, the selected model stock, recognizer CPU arena off only, and recognizer memory pattern off only. LID allocation settings remain stock. Stage two uses FP32 Parakeet beam eight with the fixed dictionary; stage three uses the existing single-instance Cohere configuration, including its fixed LID path. There are no combined arena/pattern changes, no dictionary mechanism acceptance and no new settings.

Each stage has sixteen laptop processes: quality-full twice for all four variants, then persistent twice for all four. Each group runs baseline, stock, arena-off, pattern-off and reverses that order for repetition two. Quality-full retains the existing three feeds, all 210 guard inputs and silence/noise checks. Persistent retains exactly two passes and the complete fixed take/endpoint suite from stage one. The endpoint references and annotations remain unchanged. Eight persistent phone processes use the same forward/reverse order. Phone guard/silence claims cite the two matching laptop quality companions and remain explicitly laptop evidence.

Laptop runs have 3,600-second scheduling deadlines; persistent phone runs have 2,400 seconds. Phone cooldown is at least 300 seconds between processes, including retries. Each stage has two laptop and two phone retry slots. Only one transient laptop failure before any complete output can earn a retry per stage; a second such failure stops that stage. Only complete, immediately validated phone evidence with measured thermal status above 2 can earn a thermal retry, at most once per configuration. Resource/quality negatives cannot earn retries. Timeout, invalid coverage/provenance, unexpected normalized allocation text, unavailable device, denied action or failed decoder requires handback. A deadline does not terminate a device process; active reservations persist until exit is explicitly confirmed.

The concrete whole-loop budget is 48 laptop processes (42 planned plus six retries), 24 active laptop hours, 30 phone processes (24 planned plus six retries), twelve active phone hours, and 30,000,000,000 bytes additional output storage. Stage caps remain twelve/eighteen/eighteen laptop processes, ten phone processes each, eight laptop hours and four phone hours each. The private stage records itemize planned and conservative retry audio seconds and maximum decodes. Counts include failures/timeouts. Time includes checks, build/push/pull overhead and cooldown inside an open work interval. A phone run also needs its full deadline plus the 900-second restoration margin inside the authorized window. The schedule is finite; these limits do not promise that every run fits.

Decode bounds count recognizer decode calls and Cohere's separate LID computations. Each Cohere quality process permits at most one LID call per take/feed plus each guard clip; each persistent process permits one per input/pass. The conservative decoder-audio bound includes both recognition input and an additional full-input upper bound for each LID probe; the actual first-chunk probe can be shorter. Warm-up and silence/noise remain in the harness audio bound. Stage-one legacy counts are retained and labelled as ASR calls; it has no LID model. This accounting changes no input or decoder behavior.

## Scheduler and continuation

`tools/allocation_execution.py` never executes a returned command. After an explicit launch authorization exists, use `acquire --owner SESSION`, then `next --owner SESSION`. A `begin-work` step supplies the stage/platform; open it using `begin-work --run STAGE --platform PLATFORM --owner SESSION`. Phone work additionally needs `--window PRIVATE_AUTHORIZATION_JSON`, with the same fresh-session, dated Europe/Zurich and restoration fields as the legacy launch record. Authorizing a window is separate from preparing a manifest.

The combined launch record additionally names `experiment: "allocation-v1"` and `authorized_stages: [1, 2, 3]`; a stage-one-only authorization cannot expand into this loop. Each phone-window record names the same experiment and the stages explicitly authorized inside that window. The private manifest’s `source_commit` pins the committed setup; an uncommitted or different frozen source fails full readiness. Do not refreeze after any execution event.

For each returned run, reserve it with `start --run RUN_ID --owner SESSION`, execute its frozen commands serially, then call `finish --run RUN_ID --outcome OBSERVED --owner SESSION` only after process exit. Preserve returned phone bytes with its concrete record command. Execute the immediately returned validation and comparison commands. `retry --run PREDECLARED_ID --owner SESSION` selects an eligible unused slot and retains the original attempt.

`next` returns a bounded cooldown when needed and an explicit partial handback when count, time, storage or window limits prevent another complete run. Record administrative deviations and external failure/handback reasons with `note --note REASON --owner SESSION`; never put transcript content in notes. They remain append-only beside every attempted process and restoration attestation in the aggregate report.

`next` reevaluates prior saved evidence and barriers before downstream inference. Stage one advances only with two complete independent persistent phone repetitions, valid counters/coverage and identical normalized text both between processes and between their internal passes; missing speech output stops it. Historical resource failures remain valid diagnostic evidence. Stage two advances despite valid negative allocation results, but allocation variants must reproduce stock normalized full-take, endpoint, guard and silence output exactly. Stage three completes under the same rule. There is no adaptive mode switch or gain threshold.

At a window or session boundary, finish validation and confirm all processes exited, then `pause-work --owner SESSION`. For phone work, add `--restoration PRIVATE_COMPLETION_JSON` containing `completed: true`, the authorized restoration owner's name, and the observed `final_state`. That is an explicit owner's attestation, not device verification by the helper. A new phone interval requires a new immutable authorization record with fresh checks. Ownership remains held during a planned pause; `release` is allowed only with no active process/work. Reacquisition never resets the first storage baseline, consumed time, counts, retries or evidence. No stale-owner takeover is provided.

The advisory host lock and chained append-only `execution-events.jsonl` coordinate callers of this helper. They cannot prevent unrelated tools from running. Checkout/source/model stability and exclusive execution ownership remain launch responsibilities. A pause excludes overnight time from active work; an unclosed interval continues spending time. Changing a contract after the first event fails closed.

## Reporting and readiness

`tools/allocation_report.py` implements concrete single-run, comparison, stage and aggregate reporting. `next` returns the aggregate handback on completion; save that returned JSON exclusively under `reports/`, or invoke the aggregate command with the effective replacement mapping when retries were used. The report retains per-language/term/non-term quality, guard/silence diagnostics, all endpoint waits and lexical checks, current/high-water memory, cold load, thermal readings, battery percentage where exposed, independent historical resource verdicts and evidence completeness for every repetition. CPU/profile availability and unexposed temperature/energy or other components remain explicit; coarse battery percentage does not establish energy cost.

Ranking is frozen as descriptive matched-platform tradeoffs over errors, current/high-water memory, cold load, endpoint median/maximum/all waits and thermal status. Exact normalized text establishes allocation transparency. An allocation setting has observed dominance only when both repetitions are non-worse in every comparable resource dimension, with at least one strictly lower value; a mix of positive/negative deltas is a tradeoff. Phone thermal readings are required; laptop heat is explicitly unavailable. These are descriptive point comparisons, with no statistical or meaningful-gain claim. No meaningful-gain threshold, weighted score or new product/mechanism acceptance rule is approved. A tradeoff or unresolved recommendation is a valid handback. The recording trigger is unchanged and not evaluated by this loop.
