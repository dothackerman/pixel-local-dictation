# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

The owner of a GrapheneOS Pixel 10 Pro XL, dictating in German and English through FUTO Keyboard.

## Product Purpose

Private, local dictation with fewer wrong words at an acceptable Stop wait. Dictionary management must be quick enough for everyday use.

## Operating Context

FUTO's microphone key opens a take. Stop inserts the result once; Cancel inserts nothing. Settings owns dictionary management; recording uses the saved dictionary automatically.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No network permission or account. Personal dictionary entries, recordings, transcripts and signing credentials stay out of Git. One bilingual dictionary supports words and multi-word terms without language selection. Existing takes retain their original dictionary; changes apply to subsequent takes. Historical experiment identities remain immutable.

## Product Principles

- Keep recording focused on recording.
- Make adding and correcting terms quick, with immediate per-entry saving.
- Make recovery easy; failures preserve the previous usable configuration.
- Describe measured recognition gains honestly; arbitrary new entries are not accuracy-validated.
