Crate B2

The approved direction: kraft card printed in two inks, red-orange and rind green, with Big Shoulders Display and Public Sans. It is one fixed light scheme for the panel and every screen. The chat and keyboard belong to other apps and stay dark, so the frames show the light panel against them as it will really look. Two proposals need your decision; they are listed below.

Decisions needed from you

  1. Undo for about 8 s after "Discard take" (frame "Take discarded · Undo"). If you decline, Discard stays final as it is today. It is protected only by the two-step Back → paused choice, and Error prevention drops by about one point.
  2. The "Marks explained" sheet (History menu, and a tap on the detail line). If you decline, the markers are explained only by the detail sentence and TalkBack, and the detail line is no longer tappable.

Recording panel

The microphone button looks the same in every state: a red-orange disk with a white glyph. The state shows in the glyph (microphone or Stop), the ring (hairline at rest, soft red-orange ink while recording, reaching outward unevenly with loudness) and the status line. The status row's trailing slot carries a History marker only when nothing else on the panel shows the state (today: Unable to insert text). The preview always shows 3 lines, newest last; at 2× older lines clip at the top.

History and Settings

A take with an exception gets one glyph, in one slot under its time. Rows without an exception carry nothing. The words are in the detail line, in TalkBack, and in "Marks explained" in the History menu.

Icon review: Material Symbols Sharp or custom

Each glyph was judged without words at 24dp and at font scale 2, against the other glyphs, and against the square-ended B type. The family is Material Symbols Sharp, FILL 0, wght 400, GRAD 0, opsz 24 (Apache 2.0), bundled as vector drawables. The transport buttons (play, stop) are solid, as in every Material style. Everything else is outlined at 2dp. The one exception: the uncertain question mark uses a 3dp stroke, because a thin bare question mark reads as a placeholder. Transport glyphs never appear in the status or marker slot. The glyphs on this page are hand-drawn approximations on the 24dp grid. The shipped drawables come from the official set.

Marker rules

  • One slot. Each History row has one 24dp slot under the time. On the panel the same marker goes in the status row's trailing slot, but only when the ring or arc does not already show the state. In the detail it starts the one plain exception line.
  • At most one marker per row, by priority: in progress, then cut short, then unable to insert, then uncertain. "Not attempted" and "not inserted" share a marker.
  • Colour. Markers are drawn in onSurface. Only the recording marker is red-orange, and its shape differs too.
  • Words. Each marker has exactly one sentence, used for the detail line, TalkBack and the "Marks explained" sheet.
  • A row reads as one node, e.g. "14:05, Reminder to return…, unable to insert text". Tap opens the detail; touch-and-hold copies. TalkBack offers "Copy transcript" and "Delete". The trash icon is the only nested target.
